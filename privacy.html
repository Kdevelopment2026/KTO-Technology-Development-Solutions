01 Privacy

What happens to what you send me.

This site has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. One page collects anything at all, and that is the enquiry form. Everything below describes only that.

What the form collects

Six fields: your name, your organisation, your email address, what you need, your deadline, and a description of the audience and the problem. Name, email and the description are required; the rest are optional and the form works without them.

There is one hidden field, named company-website, which is left empty by anyone using a browser normally. It exists to catch automated spam, which fills every field it finds. If it arrives filled, the submission is discarded.

Who processes it

The form is handled by Netlify, which hosts this site. Submissions are stored in a Netlify account and forwarded to me by email. Netlify is a United States company and processes data on infrastructure outside the UK, under the standard contractual clauses and the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

That is the only third party involved. Nothing else on this page loads from anywhere else: the fonts, the animation libraries, the images and the video are all served from this site rather than from a content delivery network, specifically so that visiting it does not disclose your IP address to anyone but Netlify.

What it is used for

Answering your enquiry, and nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list, not used for marketing, and not sold, shared or passed to anyone. If your enquiry becomes a project, the correspondence becomes part of that project's records in the ordinary way.

The lawful basis is legitimate interest — you contacted a business to ask about its services, and replying is what you asked for.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. Your browser stores nothing from this site beyond its ordinary cache of the files it downloaded to display the page.

If analytics are added later, this page will say so before they are switched on, and anything requiring consent will ask for it.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not become work are deleted once the conversation has clearly ended, and at the latest after twelve months. Enquiries that become projects are kept for as long as the project records are, and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it — including immediately, and including before I have replied. Email kayodefashola@hotmail.com and say which. No particular wording is needed and there is no charge. I will respond within one month, which is what the UK GDPR allows, and usually much sooner.

If you are not satisfied with how I have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab).
